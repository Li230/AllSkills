# 示例：《雨夜面摊》T2VA 15s（粒度标准落地范本）

对应 SKILL.md「第二步半」量化标准：8 镜、切镜间隔 1.6-2.4s、每镜五槽模板、因果链+道具连续+音桥。

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Cinematic, extreme wide shot, slow dolly forward at street level. A narrow rain-soaked old-town alley at night, wet cobblestones mirroring a single warm lantern. Steady drizzle streaks through the lantern light. At the far end, a tiny noodle stall glows amber, steam rising from its pot into the cold blue air. No people visible yet; the stall's canvas roof trembles under raindrops. [Shot 2] At 00:01.800, the camera cuts to a medium shot from inside the stall. An elderly man in his 60s, weathered face, grey stubble, faded navy apron over a rolled-sleeve white shirt, ladles broth into a ceramic bowl. The gas flame under the pot flares orange as he lifts the ladle, throwing moving light across his forearms. Steam drifts past his chin. He pauses mid-pour and glances toward the alley entrance. [Shot 3] At 00:03.600, the camera cuts to a full shot of the alley. A young woman in her early 20s, shoulder-length black hair soaked at the tips, cream cardigan darkened with rain, runs toward the stall holding a clear plastic umbrella low against the wind. Her steps split the puddle reflections of the lantern. [Shot 4] At 00:05.300, the camera cuts to a low close-up tracking her feet and hem. Rain boots strike shallow water; the mirrored lantern shatters and reforms with each step. The umbrella's edge drips a thin silver line across the top of the frame. [Shot 5] At 00:06.900, the camera cuts to an over-the-shoulder shot from behind the stall's hanging plastic sheet. Through its wet, distorted texture, the old man is visible still holding the half-filled bowl, now watching her approach. His ladle hand lowers. Rain drums on the canvas above. [Shot 6] At 00:08.700, the camera cuts to a medium two-shot at the cart's side. She closes the umbrella, taps it twice against the ground, and leans it against the cart's post. The old man sets the bowl on the counter and slides it toward her without a word. Steam curls up between their faces. [Shot 7] At 00:10.500, the camera cuts to a close-up across the counter. Her chilled hands wrap around the warm bowl; her shoulders drop as she exhales. The old man turns back to the pot and refills the ladle, the flame flaring once more. The lantern light steadies. [Shot 8] At 00:12.600, the camera cuts back to the extreme wide of Shot 1, slowly pulling away down the alley. The two figures shrink into the amber glow, her umbrella dripping beside the cart, rain thinning to a drizzle. The lantern sways gently, its reflection stretching across the emptying puddles until the frame settles.
overall_soundscape: Persistent soft rain on canvas and cobblestone underpins everything. A metal ladle scrapes the pot rim, broth pours with a low glug, the gas flame breathes in short flares. Footsteps splash closer through Shots 3 and 4, an umbrella snaps shut and knocks wood in Shot 6, and the scene returns to steady rain and a faint simmer.
non_diegetic_music: A sparse solo piano melody, slow tempo, enters quietly in Shot 1 and repeats its phrase with a soft string pad underneath, warming slightly as the bowl is handed over, then thinning back to solo piano as the camera pulls away, ending unresolved on a single held note.
